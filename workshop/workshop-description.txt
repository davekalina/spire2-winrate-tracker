Winrate Tracker tracks your winrate over time!  Amazing!

Features include:
- Shows reports based on actual run history per-profile and per-ascension.
- Calculates total winrate and splits (per-month, per-patch, per-10 game samples, per-50 game samples).
- Per-character and time period filters.
- Per-card and per-relic winrates.
- Time of day splits (I'm better in the evening, which is odd!)
- Charts and graphs!  They're not that exciting, really, but they exist.
- Can optionally exclude floor 1 abandons.

Project source on Github here:  https://github.com/davekalina/spire2-winrate-tracker

Winrate Tracker currently works with version 0.111.0 on the beta-branch only.
